The product

The game and the system behind it.

Sudoku Battle is designed as a full product rather than a single game mode. It combines classic Sudoku logic with live competition and persistent progression.

The technical challenge is the mix of turn-sensitive gameplay, synchronized PvP, solo modes and live-service style features such as daily content and ranking.

▣

Synchronized PvP

Live 1v1 Sudoku matches with synchronized state, friend challenges and competitive tension.

▣

Ranked progression

Dedicated ranked mode, rating progression and leaderboard-driven replay value.

▣

Solo content

Classic difficulty ladder, daily challenge, marathon mode, stats and theme customization.
