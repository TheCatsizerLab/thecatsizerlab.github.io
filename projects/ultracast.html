The product

The product and the system behind it.

UltraCast focuses on browsing and playing a user’s own media sources cleanly across different device types. The app supports playlists and organizes live TV, movies and series in a single product.

The interesting engineering surface is not only playback. It also includes navigation for TV devices, favorites, EPG handling, MultiView, casting and premium feature gating.

▣

Multi-device UI

Designed for phone, tablet, Android TV and Google TV with touch and remote control in mind.

▣

Playback features

▣

Advanced viewing

Google Cast support, optional Picture in Picture and MultiView for following several streams at once.
