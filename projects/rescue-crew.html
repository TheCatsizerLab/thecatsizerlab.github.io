Rescue Crew

Guide a crew of meerkats to safety by assigning jobs and changing the terrain. Build a crossing, dig a new route, or use a rope to get the crew to a shelter.

Product details

Assign jobs

Choose who builds, digs, climbs or places a rope. Each job changes the routes the crew can use.

Use the terrain

Shape earth and timber, then use the weight of moving objects to operate mechanisms and open crossings.

Reach the shelters

Plan routes through a 54-level campaign. Some puzzles ask you to meet separate rescue targets at multiple shelters.
