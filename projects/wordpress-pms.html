The guest journey, in 54 seconds

Read the video transcript

The video introduces La Maison des Oliviers, a demonstration holiday rental in Corsica. A search for 22–24 November 2026 and two adults returns two available nights with a total price of €300. A second search for 25–27 November returns unavailable dates and invites the guest to choose another stay. The interface is shown in French, Italian and English, followed by the responsive mobile layout. Design and development by Giovinazzo José. This demonstration property does not create reservations or take payments.

A small integration, a complete experience

Live PMS connection

A custom WordPress/PHP connector queries Beds24 for the selected dates and occupancy, then displays availability and the total stay price.

A considered interface

Property photography, a focused search form and clear result messages create a coherent journey on desktop and mobile, in three languages.

Scope of the demo

Design, development and integration by Giovinazzo José. The page uses a demonstration property for availability and pricing consultation. No reservation or payment is created.
