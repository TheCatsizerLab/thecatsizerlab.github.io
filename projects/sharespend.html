The product

The product and the system behind it.

ShareSpend is built to reduce the friction of shared expenses while also giving each user a personal budgeting layer. The app bridges expense entry, group coordination and lightweight finance tracking.

Its core value comes from combining privacy-aware receipt scanning with practical group flows such as invitations, settlements, notifications and real-time balances.

▣

Local receipt OCR

Receipt parsing is handled with Google ML Kit so photos can be processed locally on-device.

▣

Shared expense flows

Supports groups, multiple participants, settlements, who-owes-who tracking and expense history.

▣

Budget tracking

Adds personal budgets and alerting so the product works both for groups and solo finance tracking.
